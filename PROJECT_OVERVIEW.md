# Markly Project Overview

## What Markly Is

Markly is a SaaS platform for visual document review. It helps individuals and teams upload documents, mark them up directly, leave contextual feedback, and share them with others through simple review links.

In short, Markly brings the ease of sketching and commenting on a document into a collaborative online workflow.

## What The Product Is About

Markly is built for teams that regularly review documents and need a faster, clearer way to give feedback. Instead of long email threads, scattered notes, or confusing back-and-forth comments, Markly keeps feedback directly attached to the document itself.

The product focuses on making reviews:

- visual, so feedback is easy to understand
- collaborative, so multiple people can take part in the same review flow
- async, so work can move forward without meetings
- simple, so reviewers can open a link and respond quickly

## The Problem Markly Solves

Many teams still review documents through email attachments, messaging apps, screenshots, or generic comment tools. That creates friction:

- feedback gets lost across different channels
- reviewers explain issues in words instead of pointing to exact places
- approvals take longer because context is missing
- non-technical stakeholders find traditional review tools harder to use

Markly solves this by turning document review into a visual, shared experience where comments, highlights, and annotations live in one place.

## Who Markly Is For

Markly is useful for any person or team that needs structured feedback on documents, especially:

- design and creative teams reviewing visual assets and presentations
- legal teams reviewing contracts and formal documents
- architecture and construction teams reviewing plans and drawings
- product and operations teams reviewing specs, proposals, and reports
- agencies and consultants collecting client feedback

## Core Value Proposition

Markly helps teams review documents faster and with less confusion.

Its main value is that people can:

- upload a document
- annotate it visually
- leave comments tied to exact spots
- share it with others instantly
- collect feedback in one organized flow
- export a final reviewed version when the work is complete

## How Users Experience Markly

The Markly experience is centered around a simple review journey:

1. A user uploads a document.
2. They add visual feedback such as highlights, shapes, notes, or comments.
3. They share a review link with teammates, clients, or stakeholders.
4. Reviewers open the document, understand feedback in context, and respond.
5. The team reaches alignment faster and moves the document toward approval or final delivery.

## What Makes Markly Different

Markly stands out because it combines the clarity of visual annotation with the convenience of lightweight sharing.

Key differentiators include:

- document-first collaboration instead of scattered feedback channels
- visual annotations that feel natural and intuitive
- link-based review that reduces friction for external reviewers
- support for both internal teamwork and client-facing feedback
- a workflow designed around clarity, speed, and sign-off

## Business Positioning

Markly can be positioned as:

- a document review and approval platform
- a collaborative PDF feedback tool
- a client review workflow solution
- a modern alternative to email-based document review

## Short Product Summary

Markly is a collaborative SaaS for reviewing documents visually. It allows teams to upload documents, annotate them, collect contextual feedback, share review links, and move faster from draft to approval without the confusion of disconnected feedback.
